4-Cylinder V4 Engine

After finishing a 30-day Fusion 360 fundamentals course, I designed this V4 engine assembly completely on my own to prove I could apply what I learned. Modeling, assembling, and animating it took about 5 hours in total.

What I modeled

I modeled every moving part myself: the piston heads, connecting rods, crankshaft, and the wrist pins that join each piston to its connecting rod.

How it moves

I used pivot (revolute) joints and component restrictions so each part moves the way a real engine would. Turning the crankshaft drives all four pistons through their strokes.
