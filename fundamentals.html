Fundamentals

Before I build bigger projects, I want a solid base. This page tracks the fundamentals I am learning, how I am learning them, and what I have built to practice each one.

CAD: Fusion 360

I started with a 30-day Fusion 360 fundamentals course covering sketching, solid modeling, assemblies, joints, and animation. To test what I learned, I designed an inline-4 engine on my own, which is where the basics turned into real skill.

  • See the Inline-4 Engine

Electronics

Next, I am learning electronics basics with Arduino: circuits, components, reading schematics, and getting hardware to respond to code. Projects will be added here as I complete them.

Programming: C++

I am also learning C++, the language used to program Arduino boards. I will use it to control sensors and motors, and it connects directly to the electronics work above. Practice projects will be added here.

Where this is going

These fundamentals all lead toward a larger goal: building a thrust-vectoring rocket lander. CAD handles the design, electronics handles the hardware, and C++ handles the control.
